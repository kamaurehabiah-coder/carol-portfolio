about me!

Hey! I'm Carol :)

welcome to the part of the website where i'm supposed to talk about myself. i'm a student who loves noticing the little things — the way light falls across a desk after class, the colours in a busy hallway, or a quiet moment under the trees.

i enjoy photography and design. i like making things that feel soft, personal, and a little bit nostalgic. still figuring a lot of things out, but i know i want to keep creating and sharing what i see.

right now i mostly shoot with my phone and edit on free tools (Canva, Snapseed, Lightroom mobile). i also dabble in simple poster layouts, colour studies, and digital scrapbook pages.

thanks for stopping by — i hope you enjoy looking around ♡
